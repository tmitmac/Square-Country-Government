Privacy Policy

At Square Country, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

  • Personal information you provide (e.g., name, email via contact forms)
  • Non-personal information collected automatically (e.g., browser type, IP address)

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested services
  • To improve our website and services
  • To send occasional updates and announcements (with your consent)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties except as necessary to provide services or as required by law.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not function properly.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us using the information on our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with the date of the latest revision.
