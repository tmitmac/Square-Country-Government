Who handles licensing?

Liquor licences are administered by District Councils. Applications must be made to the District Council responsible for the area where the premises or event is located.

Apply for a Liquor Licence

Important: Liquor licensing applications are handled exclusively by District Councils. Select the District Council responsible for your area to continue to its official licensing website.

District Councils are responsible for receiving and processing applications for liquor licences within their regions. This includes applications for new licences, renewals, transfers, and special licences.

Select your District Council below. You will then be directed to that council's official website, where you can find information about applications, fees, requirements, and online services.
