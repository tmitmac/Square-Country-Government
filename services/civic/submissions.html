Public Consultation & Submissions

Find current government consultations and have your say on proposed legislation, policies, regulations and other matters being considered by the Government of Square Country.

Who can make a submission?

  • Square Country citizens and residents
  • Businesses and organisations
  • Community and interest groups
  • Other interested members of the public

What can I submit?

  • Feedback on proposed legislation
  • Comments on government policies
  • Responses to regulatory proposals
  • Views on public consultation documents
  • Suggestions or supporting information

Where are submissions made?

Submissions are generally made through the government department or agency responsible for the consultation. Parliamentary submissions on bills are made through the Square Country Parliament.

Make a Submission

Important: Each consultation has its own closing date and submission requirements. Make sure you read the consultation information before submitting your response.

The Government of Square Country regularly seeks feedback from the public when developing new laws, policies, regulations and government programmes. Public consultation gives individuals, businesses, organisations and communities an opportunity to provide their views before decisions are made.

Consultations may be conducted by government ministries and agencies or by the Square Country Parliament. The consultation process may include written submissions, online forms, public meetings, surveys or other opportunities for the public to provide feedback.

Submissions on legislation

If a bill is being considered by the Square Country Parliament, members of the public may be able to make a submission to the relevant parliamentary committee. Parliamentary submissions allow the public to comment on proposed legislation before the committee reports back to Parliament.

What happens to my submission?

Government departments and parliamentary committees consider submissions when reviewing proposals. Submissions may be summarised, published or otherwise used to inform the development of legislation, policies or regulations.

If you provide a submission, you should check the relevant consultation's privacy information to understand how your personal information and submission may be handled or published.

Find current consultations

To view current consultations and find out how to make a submission, visit the Square Country Parliament website. Parliamentary consultations, bills and information about making submissions are available through the Parliament.

You will be redirected to the Square Country Parliament website.
