Scholarships

Use this service to apply for Ministry of Education scholarships. These scholarships can help cover the costs of tertiary education.

Eligibility

  • Must be able to study legally in Square Country
  • Provide proof of intentions to study
  • Provide the required documents

Required Documents

  • Completed application form
  • Proof of right to study
  • A reference
  • Any other proof required for scholarship

Where can I apply for scholarships?

Scholarships can be applied for with the Ministry of Education.

Government Scholarships

Since 1959, the Government of Square Country has provided scholarships to help students access tertiary education and pursue their academic and career goals.

Up to 95% of applicants receive some form of financial support from the Government. This assistance can help students meet the costs of essential study resources, living expenses, accommodation, and university or course fees.

Government scholarships are available to eligible students entering or continuing tertiary education. Each scholarship has its own eligibility requirements, and applicants may be asked to provide information about their study plans, educational background, and financial circumstances.

To complete your scholarship application, you must use the official online application form. Click the button below to proceed to the secure application page on the Ministry of Education’s website, where you can submit your application and supporting information.

Go to Ministry of Education

You will be redirected to the Ministry of Education secure website.
