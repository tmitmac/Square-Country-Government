School Enrolment

Find a school and apply to enrol your child in primary or secondary education in Square Country.

Who can enrol?

  • Children of compulsory school age
  • Students transferring between schools
  • Students entering secondary school
  • New students arriving in Square Country

What you may need

  • Student's full name and date of birth
  • Proof of residential address
  • Parent or legal guardian details
  • Previous school records, if applicable
  • Emergency contact information

Who handles enrolments?

School enrolments are managed by individual schools, in accordance with education legislation and policies administered by the Ministry of Education.

Enrolling your child in school

Important: Some schools have enrolment zones or limited places. Check the school's enrolment requirements before submitting an application.

Every child in Square Country has access to education through the national school system. Parents and legal guardians can apply to enrol a child at their preferred primary or secondary school.

Before applying, you should find a school that offers the appropriate year level for your child. Some schools may have an enrolment zone that gives priority to students who live within a defined area.

How to enrol

  1. Find a school that meets your child's educational needs.
  2. Check whether the school has an enrolment zone or other entry requirements.
  3. Contact the school or use its online enrolment system.
  4. Provide the required student, parent and residential information.
  5. Wait for the school to confirm the enrolment.

Enrolment zones

Some schools operate an enrolment zone. If your home address is within the school's zone, your child may receive priority for enrolment. Out-of-zone applications may be accepted where places are available.

Changing schools

If your child is already enrolled at another school and you want to transfer them, contact the new school directly. The new school will advise you about available places and any information required to complete the transfer.

Find a school

Use the Ministry of Education's school directory to find schools, view their contact details and check information about enrolment.
