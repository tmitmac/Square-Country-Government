Public Housing

Apply for public housing if you need a safe, secure and affordable home and are unable to meet your housing needs in the private market.

Eligibility

  • You must normally be a Square Country citizen or resident
  • You must have a significant housing need
  • You must be unable to meet your housing needs independently
  • Your household and financial circumstances will be assessed

Information you may need

  • Proof of identity
  • Proof of residency or citizenship
  • Household and family details
  • Income and financial information
  • Current housing information
  • Information about any accessibility requirements

Who manages public housing?

Public housing is administered by the Ministry of Housing, which works with public housing providers and local authorities to provide suitable homes to eligible households.

Public Housing Applications

Important: Applying for public housing does not guarantee that you will immediately be offered a home. Applications are assessed according to housing need and the availability of suitable properties.

Public housing provides affordable, long-term housing for individuals and families who are unable to secure suitable accommodation in the private rental or home ownership market.

When you apply, the Ministry of Housing will assess your circumstances, including your current living situation, household size, income, financial position and any specific housing requirements. This assessment helps determine whether you qualify for public housing and the level of housing assistance you require.

How to apply

  1. Complete the public housing application.
  2. Provide information about your household and current housing situation.
  3. Provide any required financial and identification information.
  4. Your application will be assessed by the Ministry of Housing.
  5. If eligible, you may be placed on the public housing register.
  6. You will be contacted when suitable housing becomes available.

Housing need assessment

Applications are assessed according to the applicant's level of housing need. Factors may include whether you are homeless or at risk of homelessness, the suitability and condition of your current home, household size, income and other circumstances affecting your ability to obtain suitable housing.

Finding a suitable home

If your application is approved, you may be placed on the public housing register. The Ministry of Housing will consider your household's needs when determining which properties are suitable. The availability of homes varies between regions and communities.

If your circumstances change

You should notify the Ministry of Housing if your household, income, contact details or housing circumstances change after you apply. Keeping your information up to date helps ensure that your application is assessed accurately.
