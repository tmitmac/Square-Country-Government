Driver Licencing

Use this service to apply for a new Square Country passport or renew an existing passport. Applications are reviewed by the Department of Internal Affairs.

Eligibility

  • You must be a Square Country resident or citizen
  • You must provide valid identification
  • Previous licence is required for renewals

Required Documents

  • Completed application form
  • Proof of residency or citizenship
  • Recent licence photograph
  • Application fee

Who handles licencing?

Driver licencing is handled by the Vehicle Registration and Licencing Agency.

Driver Licencing Application

Important: Driver licencing applications are handled by the Vehicle Registration and Licensing Agency (VRLA). Click the button below to go to the VRLA website to complete online applications.

Any resident or citizen of Square Country can apply for a driver licence. A driver licence authorises the holder to legally drive on Square Country roads, subject to the conditions and restrictions applying to their licence class.

Applicants may apply for a new driver licence, upgrade to a different licence class, renew an existing licence, or replace a licence that has been lost, stolen, damaged, or expired. Applicants may be required to provide proof of identity, residency, and any other information or documentation required by the licensing authority.

To apply for a driver licence, you must use the official online application form. Click the button below to proceed to the secure driver licensing application page.
